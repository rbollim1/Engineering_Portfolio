About

I'm a BSE Engineering / BA Economics double major at Swarthmore College (GPA 3.8, class of 2027), currently on a year abroad at the London School of Economics studying Economics and Data Science.

My engineering work sits at the intersection of robotics, biomechanics, and numerical methods. I've done robotics research at Swarthmore College (barnacle nauplii locomotion), ASU's Logos Robotics Lab (computer vision and robotic grasping with ROS), and Stanford's GPS Lab (geospatial AIS data analysis for illegal fishing detection in MATLAB).

Outside the lab, I founded the Engineers for a Sustainable World chapter at Swarthmore and led a 20+ member team to build a biodiesel fuel generator. This spring I'm joining Knight Frank Investment Management as an AI & Business Optimization intern, supporting firm-wide AI implementation across investment and ESG workflows.

→

Numerical IK solver for a 5-DOF human thumb model using a damped Jacobian pseudoinverse and null-space optimization to minimize joint motion under anatomical constraints.

→
